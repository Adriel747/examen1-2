📌 Present Progressive
AffirmativeI am / 'm visiting Paris.  ·  She is / 's surfing.  ·  They are / 're hiking.
NegativeI am not / 'm not eating.  ·  He isn't swimming.  ·  We aren't shopping.
QuestionsAre you sunbathing?  ·  Is she hiking?  ·  What are they doing?

✈️ Try a Staycation!

📍 Home · 4 students on vacation at home

Raquel and Seung are taking a quiz about their city. But they aren't at home — they're running around the city and finding the answers! The questions are on their phones. They send a text with the answer and then they get another question. It's a competition and it's really fun!

Alice is visiting museums in New York, Paris, and London. How? Online, of course! At the moment, she's looking at art in the Museum of Modern Art in New York. She's sending her collection to her best friend.

Victoria and Cody are cooking food from different countries. Victoria is cooking an Indian curry and Cody is making a Thai curry. Their dad is helping them.

Miguel and his sister are volunteering at a food bank. Stores and companies give food to the food bank, and they give it to hungry people who need it. They don't get money, but they're happy because they're helping people.
